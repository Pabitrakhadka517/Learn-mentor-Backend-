# 🚀 LearnMentor - Future Roadmap & Improvements

This document outlines the planned future enhancements for the LearnMentor platform, categorized by priority and impact.

---

## 🌟 Vision: The Ultimate AI-Powered Tutoring Ecosystem

Our goal is to transition from a booking platform to a comprehensive learning management system that leverages AI to personalize the student experience and streamline tutor operations.

---

## 📅 Phase 1: Enhanced Collaboration & Resources
**Goal:** Improve how tutors and students work together between sessions.

- [ ] **Dynamic Library:** 
    - Real file uploads for tutors (PDFs, Notes, Assignments).
    - Searchable library of materials specific to each subject.
    - Progress tracking for each resource.
- [ ] **File Sharing in Chat:**
    - Attach documents, images, and voice notes directly in the messaging interface.
- [ ] **Interactive Whiteboard:**
    - Integrated real-time drawing board (e.g., Excalidraw) for explaining complex concepts visually.
- [ ] **Session Recordings:**
    - Ability to record video sessions and store them for student review.

---

## 🤖 Phase 2: AI-Powered Intelligence
**Goal:** Use AI to make the platform smarter and more efficient.

- [ ] **Smart Tutor Matching:**
    - An AI engine that recommends tutors based on a student's learning style, past performance, and specific goals.
- [ ] **Automatic Summarization:**
    - AI-generated summaries of chat conversations and video sessions to provide students with "key takeaways."
- [ ] **AI Study Assistant:**
    - A 24/7 AI bot that can answer basic academic questions based on the platform's knowledge base when tutors are offline.
- [ ] **Exam Predictor:**
    - Analytics that predict student readiness based on their performance in practice modules.

---

## 🎥 Phase 3: Integrated Video & Classrooms
**Goal:** Bring the entire tutoring experience "in-house."

- [ ] **Native Video Conferencing:**
    - Integrated WebRTC video calls so users don't need Zoom or Google Meet.
- [ ] **Virtual Classrooms:**
    - Support for small group sessions (1 tutor, multiple students) to lower costs for students.
- [ ] **Presence System:**
    - See "Last Seen" and "Currently Studying" status to build a more active community.

---

## 📱 Phase 4: Expansion & Accessibility
**Goal:** Reach more users across all devices.

- [ ] **Mobile Application (Flutter):**
    - Comprehensive iOS and Android app with push notifications.
- [ ] **Parent Portal:**
    - Dedicated view for parents to track their children's progress, bookings, and payments.
- [ ] **Dark Mode & Themes:**
    - Customizable UI for better late-night study sessions.
- [ ] **Multiple Payment Gateways:**
    - Integration with Khalti, Stripe, and Fonepay to support international and varied local users.

---

## 🛠️ Upcoming Technical Improvements
- **Unit & Integration Testing:** Comprehensive test suite for the backend.
- **Microservices Migration:** Breaking down the monolith as the user base grows.
- **Edge Caching:** Using Cloudflare to speed up resource delivery globally.
- **Sentry Integration:** Real-time error monitoring and performance tracking.
